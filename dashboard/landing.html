Frequently asked questions

Does LeadFlow work with our existing phone number or WhatsApp Business account?

Yes. LeadFlow connects to your existing number through the official WhatsApp Business API — no need to switch numbers.

How does LeadFlow decide which leads are qualified?

Each lead is scored against criteria you set — service area, service type, urgency, and how complete the request is — so your team can focus on the leads worth pursuing.

Can a real person take over a conversation?

Yes. When a conversation is sensitive, high-value, or the AI isn't confident, it's handed off to your team in the queue for review.

How long does setup take?

Most implementations are live within a few business days, depending on the details you share about your business and services.
