LeadFlow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VeloiApp / LeadFlow, operated by Raul E Polo Jay in Bogotá, Colombia ("VeloiApp," "LeadFlow," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information in connection with LeadFlow.

LeadFlow is a business-to-business software service designed to help businesses capture, qualify, respond to, and follow up with leads and assist with appointment booking.

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through LeadFlow websites, applications, dashboards, trial sign-up processes, and related services.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • people who create or use a LeadFlow account;
  • business owners, employees, and authorized users of LeadFlow;
  • businesses that use LeadFlow;
  • individuals who submit information through a website or lead form connected to LeadFlow; and
  • individuals whose information is otherwise processed through a LeadFlow customer's use of the service.

When a business uses LeadFlow to process information about its own customers or prospects, that business may determine the purposes and means for which the information is collected and used.

In those circumstances, LeadFlow may process the information on behalf of that business.

4. Artificial Intelligence

LeadFlow uses third-party artificial intelligence services to provide functions such as lead classification, qualification, language detection, response generation, and related workflow processing.

Lead information and relevant business context may be sent to an AI service when necessary to perform these functions.

LeadFlow currently uses the Gemini API.

Our current Gemini API configuration operates under Google's Paid Services / Paid Tier. Google states that, for Paid Services, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products.

This does not mean that the Gemini service provides zero data retention. Google states that, for Paid Services, prompts and responses may be logged for a limited period for purposes such as detecting violations of applicable prohibited-use policies and complying with legal or regulatory obligations.

LeadFlow does not intentionally send Customer Data to AI services for the purpose of training a general-purpose AI model.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete. Customers remain responsible for reviewing and appropriately supervising AI-generated communications.
